Safe share Unique

Send a copy of your ID, without giving the whole number away

Banks, landlords and offices often ask for an ID copy, and that copy can travel further than you'd like. Safe share reads the page on your phone, finds sensitive numbers and covers them, the same way the official masked Aadhaar keeps only the last four digits.

  • Finds Aadhaar and Virtual ID, PAN, debit and credit card, bank account and passport numbers
  • Draw a black or blurred box over anything else: zoom in to cover exactly the right words
  • Optional stamp across the page, such as "For KYC at XYZ Bank only", so a copy can't quietly be reused
  • Your original document stays untouched; only the shared copy is covered

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it really free? Is there a watermark?

Yes, every feature is free and there is never a watermark. A few small, non-personalised banner ads keep it free; they never appear on the camera or editor screens, and there are no video or pop-up ads. If you prefer, a one-time purchase removes the ads.

Does Safe share find every number?

It finds Aadhaar and Virtual ID, PAN, card, bank account and passport numbers in most clear scans, and you can turn each kind on or off. Automatic detection can still miss a number on a blurry or unusual document, so always check the preview, and draw a box over anything else you want hidden.

Does it need the internet?

No. Scanning, cleaning up, reading text, Safe share and PDF tools all work offline. The internet is only used once to download an extra text-reading or translation language you choose, and for the ads.

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?

No. They're stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. A document leaves your phone only when you share, save or print it, and only to the place you choose.

How do I make a PDF under 100 KB?

Open the document, tap Share › More options, then choose File size › Portal preset › PDF under 100 KB (or type your own limit under "Under…"). Tap Share and the PDF is made to fit.

What happens to my documents if I change phones?

By default, a PDF copy of each document is kept in your phone's Documents folder (you can turn this off in Settings). Copy that folder to the new phone and use Restore PDF copies to bring them back into the app.
