Safe share Unique

Send the copy. Keep the secrets.

Landlords, employers, visa agents and insurers ask for copies of forms and statements, and those copies get emailed, printed and stored in places you'll never see. Safe share reads the page on your phone, finds the details that could be misused and blacks them out, leaving just enough to show it's yours.

  • Finds payment card numbers (Visa, Mastercard, Amex and more), bank account numbers and the machine-readable lines on passports and ID cards
  • Can also cover email addresses, dates, and any long number or code you choose
  • Draw a black or blurred box over anything else, such as a name, address or signature, and zoom in to cover exactly the right words
  • Optional stamp across the page, such as "For rental application only", so a copy can't quietly be reused
  • Your original stays untouched; only the shared copy is covered

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it really free? Is there a watermark?

Yes, every feature is free and there is never a watermark. A few small, non-personalised banner ads keep it free; they never appear on the camera or editor screens, and there are no video or pop-up ads. If you prefer, a one-time purchase removes the ads. There's no subscription.

What can Safe share hide automatically?

Payment card numbers, bank account numbers that are labelled as such, and the machine-readable lines on passports and ID cards. You can also switch on email addresses, dates and any long number or code. Automatic detection can miss a number on a blurry or unusual document, so always check the preview, and draw a box over anything else you want hidden, such as a Social Security or national ID number.

Does it need the internet?

No. Scanning, cleaning up, reading text, Safe share and the PDF tools all work offline. The internet is only used to download an extra reading or translation language once, if you choose one, and for the ads.

Are my documents uploaded or stored anywhere else?

No. They're stored only in the app's private storage on your phone, and the app has no servers of its own. A document leaves your phone only when you share, save or print it, and only to the place you choose.

How do I make a PDF under 1 MB (or any size)?

Open the document, tap Share › More options, then choose File size › Portal preset and pick a size, or type your own limit under "Under…". Tap Share and the PDF is made to fit.

What happens to my documents if I change phones?

By default, a PDF copy of each document is kept in your phone's Documents folder (you can turn this off in Settings). Copy that folder to the new phone and use Restore PDF copies to bring them back into the app.
